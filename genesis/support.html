Your progress

Your species is saved on your device. To move it to another device, open Settings → Show save text, copy the text, and on the other device paste it under Or paste save text → Review imported save. Importing shows what you are about to load and keeps a backup of the species it replaces.

Research and away time

Research and dormant spores run on the real clock, even while the game is closed.
